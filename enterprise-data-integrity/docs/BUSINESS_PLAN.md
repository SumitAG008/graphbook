# Enterprise Data Integrity Control Room - Business Plan

## Executive Summary

**Product:** Enterprise Data Integrity + Automation Control Room

**One-liner:** "We prevent HR/Finance integration failures and bad data before they hit payroll, reporting, or compliance."

**Market Opportunity:** Large enterprises spend millions on HR/Finance/IT systems (SAP, SuccessFactors, Workday) but lack proactive data quality monitoring. Bad data causes payroll errors, compliance violations, and integration failures.

**Business Model:** Consulting-to-SaaS conversion
- Phase 1: $15K-$50K assessment engagements
- Phase 2: $2K-$10K/month SaaS subscriptions
- Target: 20 customers by end of Year 1

## Problem Statement

### The Pain
Companies with complex HR/Finance/IT ecosystems face:

1. **Payroll errors** - Wrong salaries, missed bonuses, incorrect deductions
2. **Compliance risks** - SOX violations, audit findings, regulatory issues
3. **Integration failures** - One bad record breaks entire workflows
4. **Executive distrust** - Reports don't match, decisions based on bad data
5. **Manual firefighting** - Teams spend hours debugging data issues

### Current Alternatives (and why they fail)

**Manual Excel validation:**
- Too slow, error-prone, not scalable
- Reactive, not proactive

**Enterprise data quality tools (Informatica, Talend):**
- Too expensive ($100K+ annually)
- Too complex (6-12 month implementations)
- Overkill for most teams

**Custom scripts:**
- Fragile, undocumented
- No executive visibility
- No audit trail

### Our Unique Position
- **Simple:** Upload CSV, get report in minutes
- **Affordable:** Start at $15K vs $100K+
- **Fast:** Value in 30 days, not 6 months
- **Focused:** HR/Finance integrations, not generic ETL
